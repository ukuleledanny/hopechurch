# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Local residents in Madison, AL and the broader Huntsville metro who are:
- Considering visiting Hope Church for the first time (primary audience)
- Current congregants looking for service info, sermon access, or ministry details
- Families in any life stage looking for a church community

Context: They are evaluating whether this church is the right fit for them. The site is their first impression. They are comparing it, consciously or not, against every other church site they've seen.

## Product Purpose

Hope Church's website communicates the identity, values, and practical information of the church to prospective visitors and current members. Success means a visitor leaves the site with a clear sense of who Hope Church is — and enough trust and warmth to show up on Sunday.

## Brand Personality

Reverent, grounded, serious. The tone is the weight of Scripture in a local, approachable room — not a broadcast stage, not a civic office. Unhurried. Genuine.

## Anti-references

- **Megachurch production machine**: Elevation, Life.Church, Hillsong, Bethel — the concert-stage aesthetic, cinematic production value, laser-lit hero sections. Hope is a local church, not a brand franchise.
- **Generic nonprofit template**: The lifeless stock-photo + cream-background pattern found on civic orgs, local nonprofits, and auto-generated church templates. Earnest but indistinguishable.
- **Denominational institutional**: Overly formal, top-heavy copy, ministry-org hierarchy. Cold and bureaucratic.

## Design Principles

1. **Substance over spectacle** — every design choice should serve the content, not perform production value. Quiet confidence beats bright energy.
2. **Local over broadcast** — the physical community in Madison should feel real and specific, not a scalable brand impression.
3. **Earned trust** — theological seriousness should come through in structure and restraint, not in stock photography of raised hands.
4. **Clarity of invitation** — the primary action (come visit on Sunday) should always be obvious and unhurried.
5. **Restraint as voice** — purposeful editing, not minimalism for its own sake. Every element should earn its place.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

General best practices. Legible contrast, keyboard-navigable navigation, meaningful alt text. No formal WCAG compliance target, but the congregation spans all ages — older visitors should have no trouble reading the site.
